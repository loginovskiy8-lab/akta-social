# Product facts a post may state

A post may claim only what is on this list. If an idea needs a claim that is not here,
drop the idea. When the product changes, this file is updated by hand.

## What Akta CRM is

- A CRM for Polish immigration law firms (`kancelarie imigracyjne`) and sole practitioners,
  built for `sprawy cudzoziemców` — not a renamed generic CRM.
- Staff of the firm work in one shared workspace: clients, cases, documents, tasks, deadlines,
  correspondence, employers and reports in one place.
- The status quo it replaces: case status in Excel, documents on a shared drive, arrangements in an inbox.

## Cases and documents

- 18 case types, including work permits type A–E and seasonal work.
- Case stages that follow the real course of a proceeding; each stage has its own checklist of steps.
- The firm can define its own case types and the stages for each type.
- Document catalogue and a document requirement checklist per case type; the case shows what is missing.
- Ready application packages (`gotowe pakiety wniosku`): readiness tracking, PDF generation,
  manual verification, version history.
- Folder structure created per case.

## Deadlines and work

- Calendar for office deadlines, `wezwania` and office visits.
- Dashboard: active cases, cases waiting for documents, deadlines in the next 14 days, active and overdue `wezwania`.
- Tasks and kanban boards; "Moja praca" shows a person's own cases, tasks and `wezwania`.
- Reports; the case funnel shows active cases by stage.
- Change log on records: who changed what and when.

## Clients, employers, mail

- Client register with search.
- Client portal: the client logs in separately, sees only their own case and never internal notes,
  and can send missing files. "Client" here is the foreigner whose case it is: the firm turns the
  portal on per client record. **Employers have no portal access** — never tell an employer or HR
  reader that they can log in or upload through the portal; at most, their employee can.
- Employer register: company data pulled by NIP from the `Biała lista VAT`, KRS and CEIDG, with a preview before saving.
- Gmail integration: the mailbox is synchronised into the CRM; messages are bound to a case or a client
  and can be read and answered without leaving the system.

## Team

- Roles `owner`, `admin`, `lawyer`, `assistant`; each user sees what their role permits.
- Inviting colleagues to the workspace.
- Interface in Polish, English, Ukrainian and Russian, chosen per user.

## Starting and leaving

- Self-service registration; the workspace is created after e-mail confirmation.
- Free trial: 30 days of full access, then 30 days read-only for export. No automatic payment, no automatic renewal.
- Import from CSV/Excel with column mapping, preview and duplicate rules. The team helps import data
  from Excel or another system before the start.
- The owner can export everything at any time; leaving does not need the vendor's consent.

## Data protection

- The data processing agreement (`umowa powierzenia`, DPA) and its annexes are published and can be read
  before registration (`aktacrm.com/dpa`). The DPA is concluded at registration.
- Data is processed in the EU: the database in an EU region, documents in private storage under EU jurisdiction.
- Two-factor authentication is available.

## AI

- AI assistant answering questions about cases, clients and statistics, plus AI summaries on case,
  client and employer cards.
- Off by default; the owner or an admin turns it on for the whole firm.
- PESEL, document numbers, addresses, free-text notes and health or criminal-record data are stripped
  before anything reaches the model. Processing in the EU; the model keeps neither conversations nor summaries.
